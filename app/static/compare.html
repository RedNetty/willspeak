    How this works

    The standard model is Parakeet-TDT 0.6B v2, a speech recogniser trained on typical speech. Each speaker's model started from it, was trained on other speakers with severe dysarthria, then on that speaker's own training recordings. Every recording here is from the held-out test set: none of these prompts was in that speaker's training data. The list was drawn at random before the expo, so it includes recordings the models get wrong. Single words are the weak spot. What you see is the raw model output, with no phrase list.

    Try your voice records a few seconds from this computer's microphone and sends it to both models on this device. It is not saved and not scored. Each speaker's model was trained for one person's speech, so it may do worse on yours than the standard model.
